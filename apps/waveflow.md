# WAVEFLOW

 Spotify-style desktop music player for your local library. Lock-free Rust audio (FLAC/MP3/DSD up to DSD1024), 6-band EQ, ReplayGain, gapless, crossfade, synced lyrics (LRCLIB), Last.fm scrobbling, DLNA server.
